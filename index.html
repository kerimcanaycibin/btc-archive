BTC Archive

A personal tool for preserving owner-selected market research data in Google Drive.

What the application does

BTC Archive uses rclone and archive scripts to upload selected archive files, record integrity information, and download files for verification, recovery, or owner-authorized analysis. It is operated by kerimcanaycibin for personal use.

The application is being configured. Archive automation is enabled separately after authorization and verification checks.

Google Drive access

The application requests the drive.file permission. This allows access to files it creates and files the owner explicitly makes available to the application. It does not request general access to every file in the owner's Drive.

Google authorization is used by the local or server-side archive tool. This website does not collect Google credentials or provide access to archive contents.

Owner control

The owner chooses the data to archive and can revoke Google account access. The configured archive workflow does not automatically delete original source data or remote archives.

Read the privacy policy for details about access, storage, retention, and deletion.

Contact

For non-sensitive questions, contact the operator through the project issue tracker. Do not post archive data, credentials, tokens, or account information in a public issue.
