BTC Archive

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

1. Operator and purpose

BTC Archive is a personal archiving application operated by kerimcanaycibin. It stores owner-selected market research archives in the owner's Google Drive and supports integrity verification, recovery, and analysis requested by the owner. It is not offered as a public multi-user service.

2. Data accessed

The application processes files deliberately selected for archiving, their contents, and related metadata such as names, file identifiers, sizes, timestamps, checksums, and upload results. Selected data may include market observations, research datasets, and owner-selected operational records.

Google Drive access uses the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file OAuth scope. Access is limited to files created by the application or explicitly made available to it. This scope permits management, including deletion, of those files; the configured archive workflow does not automatically delete them. The application may also read Drive storage usage and quota information for capacity planning. It does not request access to Gmail or Contacts.

3. How data is used

Data is used to upload and organize archives, verify transferred content, track archive status and available capacity, and retrieve selected files for recovery or owner-authorized analysis. Archive content is not sold, used for advertising, or used by BTC Archive to train general-purpose AI models.

4. Storage and security

Archive files are stored in the owner's Google Drive. Source files, temporary archive copies, transfer logs, and integrity records may also remain on owner-controlled computers or servers. OAuth credentials are stored in restricted configuration files used by the archive tool, not in this public website or its source repository.

Transfers to Google Drive use encrypted network connections. Access controls and checksum verification help protect the workflow. The application does not claim that archive files have an additional application-level encryption layer or that any system is free of security risks.

5. Sharing and service providers

Google processes archive files to provide Google Drive storage. The owner's computing providers may process files stored on those systems. Archives are not intentionally published or shared with unrelated parties by the archive workflow.

If the owner explicitly requests AI-assisted analysis or operations, the selected data needed for that task may be processed by the authorized AI service. That service's terms and the owner's account settings also apply. Merely visiting this website does not grant an AI service access to the owner's Google Drive.

GitHub Pages hosts this public website. The pages contain no application-added analytics scripts, advertising, sign-in forms, or cookies. GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy statement. Google's handling of data is described in its privacy policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Uploaded archives are retained until the owner removes them or changes the retention configuration. BTC Archive does not currently schedule automatic deletion of remote archives or original source data. Local copies and logs follow the owner's separate local retention settings.

The owner can delete archived files from Google Drive and remove local copies separately. Google Drive trash and backup retention follow the relevant service's behavior. Revoking application access does not delete files already stored in Drive or on local systems.

7. Revoking access

The owner can review or revoke BTC Archive's access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized access after existing credentials cease to be valid. The owner can also stop the archive job and remove stored credentials from the machines running it.

8. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the application's data practices change. The effective date appears above. For non-sensitive questions, use the project issue tracker. Do not include private data or credentials in public issues.
